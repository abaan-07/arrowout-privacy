Arrow Out: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Arrow Out (“the app”, “the game”) is a puzzle game published by Mohammed Abaan (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The game does not ask you to create an account. Your progress (current level, stars, coins, daily reward streak) and settings (such as sound on or off) are saved only on your own device. I do not receive or store this information on any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads (for example, to get a hint or extra lives) and ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. I do not control this collection. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads).

3. Purchases

The app may offer an optional one-time purchase (“Premium”) that removes ads and adds extra features. Purchases are processed by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details.

4. Information I do not collect

I do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files through the app.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact me and I will help resolve it.

6. Sharing of information

I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising and payment services described above, which operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Security and data retention

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. I keep no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for me to retain or delete on your behalf.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
